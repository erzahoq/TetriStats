# TetriStats Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 24, 2026**

TetriStats stores data used by its League statistics viewer and performance tracker. This may include:

* Discord user IDs.
* Associated TETR.IO usernames or account IDs.
* League scores, ratings, ranks, and related performance statistics.
* Historical performance data used for comparisons, such as estimating how close a score is to a particular rank.

TETR.IO statistics are retrieved from publicly available TETR.IO data. TetriStats stores this information only as required to provide its League tracking and comparison features.

TetriStats does not sell or share stored user data for advertising purposes.

Data is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide these features. If you wish to access, correct, or delete data associated with you, or have any privacy concerns, contact **erzahoq@gmail.com**. Please include your Discord user ID or TETR.IO username so the correct data can be identified.

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. The date at the top of this page will be changed when an update is made.
